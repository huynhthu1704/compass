# F-002 · Requirements: generate and approve

**Business intent:** Requirements with testable Given/When/Then acceptance criteria are drafted by an agent and approved by the PO/BA. · [intent](../intent/F-002.md) · [design](../design/F-002.md)

## Problem
Hand-written requirements are slow and often not testable.

## Users
- Product Owner
- Business Analyst

## Outcome metric
Every requirement has acceptance criteria and an approval (baseline).
