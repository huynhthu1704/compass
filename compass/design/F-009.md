## Approach
A new "Team floor" page under the project in the sidebar. A floor shows the seven agents as characters at desks in pipeline order (Ideation → Requirements → Design → Test design on the top row; Coding → Review → Release back along the bottom row), joined by a corridor. A "Your approval" desk collects runs that wait for the Product Owner. Summary chips, a feature filter, Pause motion and a live activity list sit around the floor. Drawn in the honey design system (Bee There brand, rolled out to every page in F-010).
